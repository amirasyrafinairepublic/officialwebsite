Our Story

Inai Republic

Beauty Rooted in Confidence

Inai Republic is a company founded in 2019 that specializes in modern henna and hair care products, aiming to enhance the confidence and life satisfaction of Muslim women. Led by Puan Syarifah Furaiha, who has a successful business background, the company strives to become a global leader in the henna and beauty industry while empowering individuals through entrepreneurship.

Vision

To become the foremost brand leader in the global henna and beauty industry, empowering dreams and leaving behind a positive legacy for a better future.

Mission

To empower millions with the beauty of healthier hair while fostering financial stability, self-confidence, and drawing closer to the values of Prophet Muhammad SAW.
